Sales Pitch
--------------------------------------------

It's Friday night. Everyone in the group chat is free, tonight's gonna be fun...**but wait**, what are you *actually* going to do? 

This is a problem I've been having with my friends. We all want to hang out, but we invariably end up doing the same things and going to the same places because we don't know what else there is to do.

That's the idea behind 'Go!'. At it's core, it's a social discovery and voting app (Currently just a webapp but I plan on putting it on the AppStore). You can think about it like Instagram Reels meets Google Maps.

When you first open the app, you're met with some onboarding data in the form of the following questions/prompts:

1. Your approximate age range
2. Your top 5 interests (eating, live music, nightlife, etc...)
3. Your current location (approximate is fine)
4. How far you're willing to travel

Once you answer those, you're presented with your 'Feed.' (It takes a couple of seconds to query the results, so please be patient. You might have to refresh the page if it's taking too long. This is something I'm working on fixing).

You can scroll through the presented activities and hover over their titles to see more information. 'Save' activities that you like to your 'Go-Tos' library. When Friday night inevitably rolls around, you can create/join a 'Group,' add the activities you found interesting and vote on them with your friends (They also add their activities).

And then it's set! No more waiting around for someone to take charge, simply **Discover, Vote and then Go!**
